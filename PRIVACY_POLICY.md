## Who we are

JobSwipe is a job-matching app operated by [YOUR NAME / COMPANY], based in
[COUNTRY]. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what control you have.

## What we collect

**Everyone**
- Email address and password (stored hashed — we never see your password)
- Name and contact details you enter
- Messages you send through the app

**Job seekers**
- Age, phone number, location, work preferences and availability
- Your CV, which you upload once and which is sent to businesses when you apply
- Personality questionnaire answers, and the trait profile calculated from them
- Your device's location (see below)
- Which jobs you swipe on, apply to, and match with

**Businesses**
- Business name, owner name, address and description
- Your premises location and the location of each job you post
- Job listings and the role questionnaire answers attached to them
- Which applicants you accept or decline

## Location data

With your permission, JobSwipe uses your device's location to:
- show you jobs near where you actually are, including when you travel
- send you notifications about nearby jobs that match your personality profile

We store your most recent location on our servers so notifications can work
when the app is closed. We do not track your movements continuously, do not
store a history of where you have been, and do not share your location with
other users. Businesses see only the distance between you and a job — never
your position.

You can refuse location permission and still use the app; you will see jobs
ranked without proximity, and you will not receive nearby-job notifications.
You can revoke permission at any time in your device settings.

## Your CV

Your CV is stored on our file storage and is sent to a business only when you
apply to one of their jobs. Businesses you have not applied to cannot see it.
You can replace or remove your CV at any time from your profile.

## Personality data

Your questionnaire answers are used solely to calculate a trait profile that
ranks jobs for you and shows businesses how well you fit a role. Businesses see
a percentage fit for roles you applied to — never your individual answers.

## How we use your data

We use it to operate the service: matching, applications, messaging,
notifications, and account security. We do not sell your data. We do not use it
for advertising.

## Who we share it with

- **Businesses you apply to** receive your profile details and CV
- **Service providers** who host the app on our behalf: our hosting provider,
  our database provider (MongoDB Atlas), our file storage provider, and Expo's
  push notification service
- **Authorities**, only where legally required

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account,
we permanently delete your profile, CV, applications, matches and messages.
Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

## Your rights

Under GDPR you can request access to your data, correction of it, deletion of
it, a copy of it in portable form, or object to how we process it. Delete your
account any time from Profile → Delete my account. For any other request,
contact us at [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS] and we will respond within 30 days.

## Children

JobSwipe is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data
from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we
will remove it.

## Security

Passwords are hashed. Traffic is encrypted in transit. Access to production
data is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable
measures to protect your information.

## Changes

We will update this policy as the app changes and revise the date at the top.
Material changes will be notified in the app.
